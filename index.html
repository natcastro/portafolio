About me

Natalie
Castro

Design with intention

I'm an Information Systems grad, and one of my focuses is technical product design. I'm passionate about making technology that feels easy, is nice to look at and is calm to use, with no stress and no overthinking.

Life is hard enough. I believe the tools we create should make people's lives more doable and easier to handle. I'm creative, and I like to think out of the box.

Empathy first

Information systems is the perfect mix of who I am: people, business and technology. I think about what people really need, and I don't build products they will never use.

Hard made simple

Some things need many steps. My work is to turn what is difficult into something simple to follow.

Calm and beautiful

I understand the infrastructure and the technology behind a product. That lets me create things that are beautiful and nice to use.
